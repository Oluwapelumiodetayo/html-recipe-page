Easy Chocolate Cake

A quick, moist chocolate cake perfect for beginners.

Recipe Details

Prep time: 15 minutes

Cook time: 30 minutes

Servings: 8

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 and 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/4 cup cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 large egg

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F). Grease a 9-inch pan.
  2. In a bowl, whisk together flour, sugar, cocoa, baking powder, and salt.
  3. In another bowl, mix milk, oil, and egg until combined.
  4. Pour wet ingredients into dry ingredients and stir until just combined.
  5. Pour batter into pan and bake for 25–30 minutes or until a toothpick comes out clean.
  6. Cool for 10 minutes, then remove from pan to finish cooling on a rack.

Tips & Notes

Tip: Do not overmix the batter — mix until ingredients are just combined.

Note: For a richer cake, substitute half of the milk with sour cream.

Nutrition Facts (approx)

Per serving: Calories ~320; Fat 12g; Carbs 50g; Protein 5g
